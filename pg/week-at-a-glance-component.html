Week at a Glance Component

Event schedule

Summary

A reusable three-day conference Week at a Glance component based on large professional conference scheduling interfaces. Displays major morning, midday, and evening program blocks across a weekend timeline, with overlapping sessions in multiple rooms and expandable program details including sessions, presentations, faculty, moderators, speakers, live cases, and locations. Designed as a technology-neutral reference implementation before selecting the final framework or language.
